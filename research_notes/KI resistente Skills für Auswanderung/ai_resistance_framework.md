# AI-Resistance Framework: Which Skills & Knowledge Work Stay Durable vs. Which Are Most at Risk (2026 and forward)

*Scope note: This briefing prioritizes globally relevant, remote-capable knowledge work. All major labor-market figures below come from employer surveys or model-based projections (WEF, McKinsey, OECD, OpenAI/academic) and are forecasts, not measured outcomes — dated accordingly. Several primary sites (weforum.org, oecd.org, arxiv.org) were DNS-unreachable during research; where that happened, figures are drawn from secondary coverage and flagged.*

---

## Key Question 1: Which skill clusters are projected to GROW and which to SHRINK through ~2030–2035?

### Takeaway
The WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 projects ~170M jobs created and ~92M displaced by 2030 (net +78M, ≈14% of today's employment), with ~39% of core skills changing by 2030. Growth concentrates in AI/tech, cybersecurity, green energy, care, and education plus human skills (creative thinking, resilience, curiosity); decline concentrates in clerical, administrative, and routine transaction roles.

### Cited Findings
- WEF projects ~170M new jobs created and ~92M displaced by 2030, a net gain of ~78M jobs (≈14% of today's employment) — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/wef-2025/story/wef-2025-170-mn-new-jobs-to-be-created-by-2030-but-92-mn-would-be-displaced-459964-2025-01-08); [itedgenews](https://itedgenews.africa/future-of-jobs-report-2025-92m-jobs-to-vanish-for-170m-new-jobs)
- Employers say roughly 39% (reported elsewhere as "nearly 40%") of current skill sets will change by 2030 — [EU Digital Skills & Jobs portal](https://digital-skills-jobs.europa.eu/en/latest/research/future-jobs-report-2025)
- 63% of employers cite the skills gap as the key barrier to transformation — [EU Digital Skills & Jobs portal](https://digital-skills-jobs.europa.eu/en/latest/research/future-jobs-report-2025)
- **Fastest-GROWING skills:** AI and big data (top), then networks & cybersecurity, then technological literacy; complemented by creative thinking, resilience/flexibility/agility, and curiosity & lifelong learning — [etradeforall / WEF coverage](https://etradeforall.org/es/node/12999)
- Skills most differentiating growing from declining roles: resource management & operations, quality control, programming & technological literacy — [etradeforall / WEF coverage](https://etradeforall.org/es/node/12999)
- **Fastest-GROWING roles (% terms):** big data specialists, fintech engineers, AI & machine-learning specialists lead; growth also concentrated in green energy, care economy, and education — [etradeforall / WEF coverage](https://etradeforall.org/es/node/12999); [malaymail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2025/01/08/wef-report-78-million-jobs-to-be-created-by-2030-urgent-need-for-upskilling-in-ai-big-data-cybersecurity-and-human-skills/162450)
- **Fastest-DECLINING roles:** postal service clerks, bank tellers, data-entry clerks, cashiers/ticket clerks, administrative assistants and other clerical roles — [etradeforall / WEF coverage](https://etradeforall.org/es/node/12999); [ippmedia](https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/features/read/wef-fastest-growing-declining-jobs-looming-large-2025-08-19-183552)
- Report methodology: >1,000 of the world's largest employers, representing >14M workers across 22 industry clusters and 55 economies; figures are employer expectations (projections) — [EU Digital Skills & Jobs portal](https://digital-skills-jobs.europa.eu/en/latest/research/future-jobs-report-2025)
- **McKinsey Global Institute ("Generative AI and the future of work in America"):** generative AI could push the share of U.S. work hours automatable to 29.5% by 2030, up from 21.5% without gen-AI acceleration; STEM professionals' automatable hours rise ~16 points to ~30%, and education/training professionals rise ~16 points to ~23% — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/accelerated-adoption-ai-automate-30-americans-work-hours-mckinsey)

### Inferences
- The durable cluster is bimodal: (a) high-skill AI/tech/cyber/green building skills, and (b) human-centric skills (creativity, resilience, relationship, judgment). Pure routine cognitive/clerical work is the squeezed middle.
- Because growth leaders (big data, fintech, AI/ML, cybersecurity) are remote-capable and global, they fit a relocating remote worker — but they are also the most competed-for.

## Key Question 2: What properties make work AI-resistant? (Framework)

### Takeaway
A consistent cross-source framework: work resists automation when it combines physical/manual execution in unstructured environments, high-trust human relationships, accountability/liability for consequences, contextual/local judgment, and cross-domain synthesis. OECD's capability mapping confirms current AI is closest to codifiable information processing and furthest from contextual judgment and interpersonal understanding.

### Cited Findings
- OECD's AI exposure measure maps AI capabilities across nine cognitive, social and physical domains: current AI is **closest to** routine information processing, administrative work, and codifiable tasks, and **furthest from** roles needing contextual judgement and interpersonal understanding — [OECD AI exposure measure (via search summary)](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-oecd-ai-exposure-measure_f3da0f0a-en.html)
- OECD: actual impact depends on adoption, regulation, organisational change and social choice (not technical feasibility alone) — [OECD AI exposure measure](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-oecd-ai-exposure-measure_f3da0f0a-en.html)
- Autor-style task framework (abstract / routine / manual): routine tasks automate first; manual tasks needing dexterity and adaptability in messy real-world settings are hard to automate (e.g., spatial reasoning to work in an attic with bad wiring) — [Extern guide](https://www.extern.com/post/jobs-ai-wont-replace-guide)
- "Accountability" test: judgment calls stay with people because someone must own the consequences of a wrong one; a decision test asks whether a machine can be held responsible, feel empathy, or handle human complexity — [Girlboss](https://girlboss.com/blogs/read/what-jobs-will-ai-not-replace-audit-your-role-in-4-steps)
- Preferences/trust may block automation for ~25% of tasks — social interaction, personal care, hospitality, childcare — though humanoid robots and new hardware could shift these limits — [rework.com AI future of work](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/ai-future-of-work/which-jobs-ai-changes-rather-than-replaces)
- OpenAI "GPTs are GPTs" (2023): roles reliant on **science and critical-thinking** skills correlate **negatively** with LLM exposure; **programming and writing** skills correlate **positively** — [ar5iv mirror of 2303.10130](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.10130); [Slator](https://slator.com/translators-interpreters-among-most-vulnerable-large-language-models-openai/)
- Anthropic Economic Index: Claude use was lowest for very-low-paid and very-high-paid jobs that require manual dexterity (e.g., shampooers, obstetricians) — [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-anthropic-economic-index)

### Inferences (synthesized framework — "6 moats")
A task/role is AI-resistant to the degree it has:
1. **Physical presence & manual dexterity in unstructured environments** (trades, nursing, PT) — robotics lags and is capital-intensive.
2. **High-trust human relationship** (therapy, sales, negotiation, care) — therapeutic/relational "alliance" needs a human on both sides.
3. **Accountability / liability / regulated sign-off** (auditors, doctors, lawyers, engineers who stamp) — someone must own consequences.
4. **Complex contextual judgment under ambiguity** — negatively correlated with LLM exposure (critical thinking/science).
5. **Local / tacit / institutional knowledge** not in training data.
6. **Cross-domain synthesis & orchestration** — connecting fields and owning the whole outcome.
Note: moats #1 and #2 are inherently in-person (less relevant for a remote worker); moats #3–#6 are the remote-capable durable cluster.

## Key Question 3: Which knowledge-work tasks are being automated FASTEST right now?

### Takeaway
Empirical freelance-platform studies (2023–2025) show the "freelance classics" — basic writing/copywriting, translation, simple coding, data entry, routine graphic/reporting — are already eroding, with demand for substitutable writing/translation skills down 20–50%. This is measured, not speculative.

### Cited Findings
- Oxford/JEBO study (2025, >3M freelance postings): demand for partly-substitutable skills (writing, translating) dropped **20–50%** vs. counterfactual trend; sharpest for short-term (1–3 week) jobs and for experienced writers/translators — [Oxford ORA](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:af491969-bee7-4b09-a696-2b500b6b21d8)
- Same study: AI-complementary work grew — chatbot and NLP jobs **almost tripled** since ChatGPT's launch — [Oxford ORA](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:af491969-bee7-4b09-a696-2b500b6b21d8)
- Qiao et al. (2024): ChatGPT release caused sharp displacement in **translation** (fewer tasks, lower average earnings); web development moved the other way (grew) — [search summary / thewrap](https://thewrap.com/study-finds-chatgpt-drives-down-freelance-work-pay)
- Liu et al. (2023): writing and translation gigs contracted post-ChatGPT; both job counts and active freelancer pool shrank, demand falling faster than supply — [search summary](https://thewrap.com/study-finds-chatgpt-drives-down-freelance-work-pay)
- Hui et al. (2024, Upwork): smaller effect — orders down 2%, monthly earnings down 5.2%; authors caution long-term may differ — [Etla](https://www.etla.fi/en/latest/winners-and-losers-of-generative-ai-in-the-freelance-job-market-etla-participated-in-an-international-research-project/)
- OpenAI "GPTs are GPTs": ~80% of U.S. workforce could have ≥10% of tasks affected; ~19% could see ≥50% of tasks impacted; translators/interpreters among most exposed; higher-wage occupations generally show higher exposure — [ar5iv](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2303.10130); [Slator](https://slator.com/translators-interpreters-among-most-vulnerable-large-language-models-openai/)
- Anthropic Economic Index: AI use concentrated in software development and technical writing; computer/math roles = 37.2% of queries; copywriters among heaviest users — [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-anthropic-economic-index)
- WEF declining roles reinforce this: data-entry clerks, administrative assistants, bank tellers, cashiers, postal clerks — [ippmedia](https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/features/read/wef-fastest-growing-declining-jobs-looming-large-2025-08-19-183552)

### Inferences
- The honest read for a freelancer: commodity text production (basic copywriting, basic translation, boilerplate coding, data entry, template graphic/reporting) is actively eroding NOW — these are the worst bets to start building in 2026.
- Caveat the report-writer should keep: these are short-term platform findings; demand is shifting toward AI-complementary niches (NLP/chatbot work tripled), not vanishing wholesale.

### Gaps
- Effects on basic graphic design are inferred from WEF/clerical decline and general commentary, not a dedicated measured study in these results.

## Key Question 4: Which human skills become MORE valuable BECAUSE of AI?

### Takeaway
Skills that supervise, orchestrate, and take responsibility for AI output rise in value: AI orchestration/oversight, quality control and validation of AI output, workflow/system design, plus durable human skills (relationship/sales, strategy, cross-domain judgment). An AI-skills wage premium is reported (PwC ~62%, up from 57%), though vendor-sourced.

### Cited Findings
- Journal of Expertise (2026) "expert as AI orchestrator": value shifts to those who can **discern and validate quality in work they may not be able to execute themselves** and stay responsible for outcomes — [Journal of Expertise](https://www.journalofexpertise.org/articles/volume9_issue2/JoE_9_2_Rousse_Scapini.pdf)
- Same paper warns entry-level pathways that historically built that judgment are being eroded by AI — [Journal of Expertise](https://www.journalofexpertise.org/articles/volume9_issue2/JoE_9_2_Rousse_Scapini.pdf)
- Workers who can **evaluate** AI outputs (not treat AI as a black box) are far more valuable; AI struggles with context, tradeoffs, accountability — [Tom's Guide (citing McKinsey/WEF)](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/is-your-job-ai-proof-10-skills-becoming-more-valuable-in-2026)
- ~60% of employees will need to upskill **beyond prompting** to supervise, audit, and trust AI systems (Cisco/Omdia, vendor-linked) — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/artificial-intelligence/7-ai-skills-that-will-matter-more-than-prompt-engineering-in-2027)
- Prompt engineering will persist but as a **baseline** skill, not a differentiator — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/artificial-intelligence/7-ai-skills-that-will-matter-more-than-prompt-engineering-in-2027)
- Reported AI-skills wage premium of ~62% (up from 57% a year earlier), attributed to PwC — unverified against PwC's own report — [Analytics Insight (citing PwC)](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/amp/story/artificial-intelligence/15-most-important-ai-skills-every-professional-needs-by-2027)
- WEF confirms demand for AI/big data, cybersecurity, tech literacy PLUS creative thinking, resilience, curiosity/lifelong learning — [etradeforall](https://etradeforall.org/es/node/12999)

### Inferences
- The highest-leverage remote skill to build in 2026 is not "produce content" but "architect, direct, and quality-control AI-driven workflows and own the result" — orchestration + domain judgment + accountability.
- Sales/relationship and strategy gain relative value because AI lowers the cost of execution, raising the premium on deciding *what* to do and winning *trust* to do it.

### Gaps
- No rigorous 2025–2026 causal study measuring the wage return to AI-oversight skills specifically; evidence is forecasts and vendor surveys.

## Key Question 5: Augmentation vs. replacement for business / finance / analyst roles

### Takeaway
For finance/analyst roles the consensus is augmentation-dominant with entry-level erosion: AI automates routine modeling, reporting, reconciliation, and sampling, shifting humans toward advisory, judgment, and risk work. Anthropic's data shows AI use overall is ~57% augmentation vs. ~43% automation (Feb 2025), trending toward more automation in later-2025 coverage.

### Cited Findings
- **Anthropic Economic Index (Feb 2025):** 57% augmentation vs. 43% automation; augmentation = task iteration (31.3%), learning (23.3%), validation (2.8%); automation = directive (27.8%), feedback loop (14.8%); **no evidence of fully automated jobs**; ~36% of occupations use AI for ≥25% of tasks, ~4% for ≥75% — [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-anthropic-economic-index)
- Business & financial occupations = 5.9% of Claude queries (mid-pack) — [Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-anthropic-economic-index)
- Later-2025 Anthropic update (secondary, unverified): shift toward ~45% automation / ~52% augmentation — [letsdatascience summary](https://www.letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-finds-ai-augments-rather-than-replaces-e57396e7)
- **Accountants/auditors:** BLS (via secondary) projects ~5% employment growth 2025–2035; AI takes routine tasks, advisory work becomes prominent; bookkeeping/accounting/audit clerks projected to **decline ~6%** — [rework.com AI for finance](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/ai-for-finance/ai-and-finance-team-roles)
- **Financial analysts:** ~7% projected growth (BLS via secondary), no AI-specific note on the role page — [rework.com](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/ai-for-finance/ai-and-finance-team-roles)
- **Entry-level pressure:** Bloomberg Intelligence projection (via secondary) that Wall Street banks could cut up to 200,000 roles, junior/entry-level analysts most exposed because routine reporting/basic modeling is what AI now does well — [mileseducation](https://www.mileseducation.com/blog/accounting/can-ai-for-financial-analysis-replace-financial-analysts)
- **Audit:** 2024 study of >400,000 resumes across 648 audit offices found AI adoption coincided with a 4.3% increase in auditor jobs; AI automates sampling/reconciliations, shifting auditors to risk and judgment — [mileseducation](https://www.mileseducation.com/blog/accounting/will-ai-replace-auditors)
- McKinsey: business/financial professionals are automation-exposed but the pattern is task-shift, not role-elimination; STEM and education professionals see the largest gen-AI-driven jumps in automatable hours — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/economy/accelerated-adoption-ai-automate-30-americans-work-hours-mckinsey)

### Inferences
- The finance/analyst split is clear: transaction processing, basic modeling, and routine reporting (entry-level tasks) are replacement-exposed; advisory, client relationship, scenario strategy, risk judgment, and sign-off are augmentation-protected.
- Structural risk the report-writer should flag: AI erodes the junior rungs that historically trained senior judgment (echoed by the Journal of Expertise orchestrator finding) — a career-entry problem even where senior roles stay durable.

### Gaps
- BLS 2025–2035 projection figures are relayed via secondary sites (rework.com, mileseducation), not verified against bls.gov; the "200,000 roles" and later Anthropic split are projections/secondary and should be hedged.

## Cross-cutting notes for the report-writer
- **Dating:** WEF Future of Jobs = Jan 2025 (horizon 2030). Anthropic Economic Index = Feb 2025 (+ unverified late-2025 update). McKinsey "Gen AI and future of work in America" horizon 2030. OpenAI "GPTs are GPTs" = 2023 (flag as earliest/foundational but dated). OECD AI exposure measure = working paper, treat as framework not fresh 2025 data.
- **Honest both-ways framing:** eroding now (measured) = basic copywriting, basic translation, boilerplate coding, data entry; durable (framework-supported) = trades/care (in-person), and remote-capable: AI orchestration/oversight, cybersecurity, complex judgment/advisory, relationship/sales, cross-domain synthesis, regulated sign-off roles.
- **For a relocating remote worker specifically:** the in-person moats (#1 physical, #2 high-touch care) don't travel well as remote work; the best remote-durable bets are the accountability + orchestration + domain-judgment cluster (AI workflow design/QC, cybersecurity, data/ML engineering, advisory finance, and trust-based sales/strategy).
